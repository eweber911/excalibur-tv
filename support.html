Getting started without a subscription

Excalibur ships with no channels. To try it with nothing of your own, open Settings → Add playlist → Free channels and pick a country. These are free-to-air channels published openly by broadcasters and indexed by the iptv-org project.

Adding your own service

Xtream needs the server address, your username and your password. M3U8 needs a playlist address, and optionally a guide address which you can add separately under Settings → Add guide.

Common problems

A channel will not start, or stops after a minute. Most IPTV accounts allow only one or two streams at a time. If another television in the house is watching, or a stream was recently closed, the provider may refuse a new one for up to a minute and a half. The app waits and retries on its own.

The guide is mostly empty. Guide data comes from your provider, and many providers supply very little of it. You can add extra guide sources under Settings → Add guide.

Nothing plays at all after adding an account. Check the address, and check whether your subscription has expired. The app reports what the provider actually said under Settings → Diagnostics.

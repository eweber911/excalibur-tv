Privacy Policy

Excalibur AI TV · SmartMax Software, Inc. · Last updated 25 August 2026

We collect nothing. Excalibur has no account system, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting service, and no server of ours for your data to be sent to. Nothing in the app phones home.

Excalibur is a player. It shows you television from a service you already subscribe to, or from openly published free-to-air channels. This policy describes what the app stores on your own device, and the three places data can travel to, all of which are yours rather than ours.

What is stored on your Apple TV

  • Your provider's address, username and password. Passwords are held in the device Keychain.
  • Your channel list and guide listings, downloaded from your provider so the app can show a schedule without asking again every time.
  • Your favorites, recently watched channels, and how far into a film or episode you got.
  • A rolling diagnostic log of playback events, kept on the device to help diagnose stalls. Addresses written to it have their credentials removed first. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Recorded and buffered video, if you turn on the live buffer or recording, stored in the app's cache on the device.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Your IPTV provider

When you play a channel, the app contacts the provider you configured, exactly as any other player would. Your username and password go to that provider because that is how they identify you. We are not a party to that relationship, we do not see it, and we have no agreement with any provider. Excalibur does not supply, resell, host, or index any channels or content.

iCloud

If you use more than one Apple TV, the app can keep your provider accounts, favorites, watch positions and guide sources in step between them. This uses your own Apple Account and your own iCloud storage. It does not pass through any service of ours, and there is no sign-in of ours involved.

One thing worth being explicit about: to make your accounts work on a second Apple TV without retyping them, provider passwords are included in that iCloud data. tvOS does not synchronize third-party Keychain items between devices, so this is the only mechanism available. The data is stored under your Apple Account and is protected by Apple's encryption; it is end-to-end encrypted only if you have Advanced Data Protection turned on. If you would rather your passwords never leave the device, turn account syncing off in Settings and enter each account on each Apple TV by hand.

Optional AI features, using your own key

Excalibur's AI features are switched off until you supply your own API key for Anthropic or OpenAI. We do not have a key, a server, or an account with either company. Requests go from your Apple TV straight to the provider you chose, billed to you, under their privacy policy. With no key entered, these features are absent and nothing is sent.

When you do use them, what leaves your device is:

  • A still image of what is on screen, when you ask the app what you are watching. This is a frame of the television programme, sent so the model can read a channel logo or a scoreboard.
  • A short summary of channels you have watched, and the time of day, when you ask for a recommendation.
  • What you type into search, when you use the AI search.

Your API key is kept in the device Keychain, is never written to a log, and is never sent anywhere except to the provider it belongs to.

What we never do

  • No tracking, across this app or any other.
  • No advertising identifier, no vendor identifier, no device fingerprinting.
  • No analytics or telemetry sent off the device.
  • No selling or sharing of anything, because there is nothing collected to sell or share.

Children

Excalibur is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone. The app includes a PIN lock for channels a provider marks as adult; that PIN is stored on the device only.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the date at the top will change and the new version will appear here before the app version that relies on it ships.
